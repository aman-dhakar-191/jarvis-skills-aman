# Skill: research-deep

Conduct structured, multi-source research with careful ranking of sources by reliability and relevance, culminating in a concise written brief.

Use when: Aman requests comprehensive and well-organized information synthesis from multiple sources with clear source evaluation.

Steps:
1. Identify relevant, credible sources across diverse platforms.
2. Rank sources based on reliability, expertise, and recency.
3. Synthesize information into a clear, concise written brief.
4. Cite key sources appropriately.

Output: A structured summary highlighting key insights backed by ranked sources.

Constraints: Avoid including unverified or uncertain information; maintain objectivity and clarity.
